# Mutual non-disclosure agreement (template)

> Template for discussion. It is not legal advice. Have counsel review it before use.

**Parties.** Techtic Solutions ("Techtic") and `[Client legal name]` ("Client"), together "the parties".
**Effective date.** `[date]`   **Term.** Two years from the effective date; confidentiality duties for trade secrets continue as long as the information remains a trade secret.

**1. Purpose.** The parties will share information so that Techtic can review Client's repair invoices and report on likely overbilling and missed recoveries (the "Purpose").

**2. Confidential information.** All non-public information a party discloses for the Purpose, in any form, including repair invoices and their contents, vehicle, shop, pricing and spend data, the audit findings and report, and either party's business and technical information. It does not include information that is public through no fault of the receiving party, was already known to it, was independently developed, or was received lawfully from a third party without restriction.

**3. Use and protection.** The receiving party will use confidential information only for the Purpose; share it only with employees and contractors who need it and are bound by written confidentiality duties at least as protective as this agreement; and protect it with at least reasonable care, including the safeguards in Techtic's Data handling policy (attached as Schedule A).

**4. Service providers.** Client agrees Techtic may process invoice data with: (a) cloud storage in the United States; and (b) an AI service provider, solely to read and analyse the invoices, under an agreement that provides zero data retention (or equivalent) and prohibits training on the data. Techtic will not send Client's data to any other third party without Client's written consent. Techtic remains responsible for its providers.

**5. Required disclosure.** If the law requires disclosure, the receiving party will give prompt notice where permitted and disclose only what is required.

**6. Return and deletion.** On request, and in any case within 90 days after the audit is complete unless Client asks for a different period in writing, Techtic will delete Client's original invoice files and page images. Extracted data and reports are kept for `[__]` months unless Client asks for deletion, after which they are deleted too. Techtic will confirm deletion in writing on request. Routine backups are deleted on their normal cycle.

**7. Security incidents.** Techtic will tell Client without undue delay (and within 72 hours of confirming it) if Client's confidential information is accessed or disclosed without authorisation.

**8. Findings.** Audit findings are estimates based on the invoices provided and industry benchmarks and are reviewed by an analyst; they are not a legal determination that any shop has acted improperly. Neither party will publish findings that name a shop without the other's written consent.

**9. No licence; no obligation.** No rights in the information are granted except for the Purpose. Neither party must enter into any further agreement.

**10. Remedies; general.** Unauthorised use or disclosure may cause harm for which damages are inadequate, so the disclosing party may seek injunctive relief. This agreement is governed by the laws of `[State]`, is the entire agreement on its subject, and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.
